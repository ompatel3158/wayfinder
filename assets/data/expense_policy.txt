Employees must exercise fiscal prudence and good judgment when incurring business expenses. All expenditures must be directly related to company business operations and strategic objectives. Personal purchases combined with business expenses will result in claim rejection.

Business travel arrangements including flights and train tickets must be booked at standard economy class. Bookings should be secured at least fourteen days in advance to minimize rate premiums. Any seat upgrades or premium class bookings require prior executive sign-off.

Lodging and accommodations must adhere to regional nightly spending caps established by the finance team. Incidentals such as mini-bar items and streaming services are non-reimbursable personal costs. Hotel folios must show detailed itemization rather than summarized lump sums.

Daily meal allowances apply to full travel days when an employee is away from their home office. Alcohol purchases are non-reimbursable unless associated with an approved client entertainment event. Detailed itemized restaurant receipts must accompany the expense report.

Software subscriptions and digital tooling require IT security clearance before procurement. Team members should first check the internal enterprise catalog for existing software licenses. Individual card purchases for unapproved cloud tools are strictly prohibited.

Expense reports must be compiled and submitted within thirty days of the transaction date. Submissions missing itemized VAT invoices or receipts will be returned to the employee. Recurring violations of expense submission procedures may lead to card suspension.
