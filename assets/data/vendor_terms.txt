Standard payment terms for all authorized vendors are Net 30 days from receipt of a verified invoice. Invoices must reference a valid company purchase order number to prevent payment delays. Disputed line items will be withheld until mutual reconciliation is achieved.

Vendors agree to maintain competitive commercial pricing and honor established master service agreement discount structures. Rate increases require sixty days advance written notice and require commercial review. Price escalations applied retroactively without consent will be rejected.

Goods and deliverables are subject to receiving inspection and quality verification upon arrival. Defective merchandise or non-conforming service deliverables must be cured within ten business days. Failure to remedy deficiencies entitles the buyer to cancel without penalty.

Confidential information disclosed during contractual engagements must remain strictly protected under non-disclosure obligations. Proprietary technical data and financial records must not be shared with outside third parties. These confidentiality duties survive termination for five years.

Contractors must maintain comprehensive commercial general liability insurance throughout the term of work. Certificates of insurance naming the enterprise as an additional insured must be submitted annually. Minimum coverage thresholds apply based on project risk classification.

Either party may terminate an active statement of work with thirty days written notice for convenience. Material breach triggers immediate contract cancellation if left uncured after fourteen calendar days. Upon termination, all work-in-progress deliverables must be transferred immediately.
